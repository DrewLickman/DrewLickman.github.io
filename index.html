Hello, I'm Drew.

I build AI-assisted automation that turns repetitive work into reliable, reviewable systems. At Deloitte and MTSU, I found workflow friction, mapped the problem, and built practical scripts and tools to reduce it.

I also build interactive entertainment: Unity gameplay systems, browser-based party games, and tools that help players explore the games they love. That mix keeps me focused on both the system behind an experience and the person using it.

  • AI automation: workflow analysis, PowerShell, local tools, and AI-assisted development
